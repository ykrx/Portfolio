# [verbalize.photos](https://ai-image-describer.web.app)

![verbalize-photos-screenshot](/assets/verbalize-photos-ss.png)

Expanding UX by involving other senses in the absence of sight.

**The Internet is heavily visual.** Over _3 billion_ images are shared over the internet _daily_ (2022), most of which are completely hidden from users who can’t physically _see_ them. Developers do not do enough to address this accessibility gap, be it by not providing descriptive alt tags, missing/poor aria-labels, and generally not thinking about accessibility of visual content.

Final project for Human Computer Interaction course at NYU. Aiming to improve the experience of the visual side of the internet for visually-impaired users. Users can paste a photo or a link to a photo, and generate a human, verbal description of the photo, using Computer Vision, Speech Synthesis, and Natural Language Processing, along with Braille integration, and “ambience” (further enhancing the imaginative experience by involving another sense of perception). Supports _voice control_, _keyboard navigation_, and _multiple languages_ (English, French, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian).

### Built with

`typescript` `react` `azure` `mui` `scss`
